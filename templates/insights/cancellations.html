The dataset has no cancellation reason field. The only defensible signal is a negative quantity on an invoice number beginning with "C", which is treated as a cancellation proxy — not proof that a customer returned the goods.
Limitation: an invoice line with a negative quantity may reflect a cancellation, a correction, or an adjustment. Missing customer IDs also mean customer-level figures cover only identified customers, so the shares below describe the recorded data, not the retailer's true return rate.
